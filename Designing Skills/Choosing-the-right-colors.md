### 🔥 3 Killer Color Combos (Tested on ThemeForest style templates):

#### Combo 1 – Luxury/Creative:

```txt
Background: white / gray-50  
Primary: purple-600  
Accent: pink-500  
Text: gray-800
```

#### Combo 2 – Clean SaaS:

```txt
Background: gray-50  
Primary: blue-600  
Accent: indigo-500  
Text: gray-700
```

#### Combo 3 – Dark Premium:

```txt
Background: gray-900  
Card: gray-800  
Text: gray-200  
Accent: emerald-400 or rose-400
```
